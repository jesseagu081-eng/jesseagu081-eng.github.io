Job Application Tracker

Problem
Every application has to be logged, acknowledged, passed to HR and sorted by experience before the right reply is sent. Done manually, this is slow and prone to error.
Solution
An n8n workflow that runs on every new application. It skips duplicates, saves the applicant, sends a confirmation email and alerts HR. It then sorts each applicant by experience: Junior (0–2 years), Mid-level (3–5 years) or Senior (6+ years). Each level is saved to its own sheet and receives the appropriate email, from a kept-on-file reply to an interview invitation.
Result
Tested with one applicant per experience level, then run live during the presentation. Every applicant was logged, emailed and filed to the correct sheet.
Platform
n8n · 12 nodes

Customer Feedback Automation

Problem
A car care chain with branches in Manchester, Leeds, Liverpool and London receives more feedback than it can review quickly. Urgent complaints are delayed, and managers have no single view of each branch.
Solution
A Make scenario that picks up new feedback, checks the customer's history and uses ChatGPT to score sentiment and severity. A router then sends each item down one of five routes, such as ready to post, private queue, manager alert or needs human review. Every route logs to Google Sheets and the management dashboard, and urgent cases also email the manager.
Result
In testing, the scenario processed feedback in 11 seconds. The dashboard showed volume by branch, average sentiment and severity, routing outcomes, and draft replies for negative feedback.
Client type
Car care chain with four branches
