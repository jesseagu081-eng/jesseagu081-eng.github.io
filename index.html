Case study 01 · Presented at TS Academy

Job Application Tracker

An automated hiring pipeline that confirms, records and sorts every job application the moment it arrives.

Platform
n8n
Workflow
12 automated steps
Starts when
A new application is received
Built with
n8nGoogle SheetsGmail

The challenge

Every application has to be logged, acknowledged, passed to HR and sorted by experience before the right reply is sent. Done manually, this is slow and inconsistent, and applicants are left waiting.

How it works

  1. Capture. A new application starts the workflow. Applications already processed are skipped, so nobody is emailed twice.
  2. Record and respond. The applicant is saved to an All Applicants sheet and receives an instant confirmation, and HR is alerted.
  3. Sort. A rules-based switch groups applicants by experience: Junior (0–2 years), Mid-level (3–5 years) or Senior (6+ years).
  4. Follow up. Each group is saved to its own sheet. Junior applicants receive a kept-on-file email; mid-level and senior applicants receive an interview invitation.

Result

Tested with one applicant per experience level, then run live during the presentation. Every applicant was recorded, emailed and filed to the correct sheet with no manual steps.

Business value

  • Instant replies. Every applicant hears back within seconds, which protects the employer's reputation.
  • Time saved. HR no longer copies details between forms, sheets and inboxes.
  • Faster hiring. Strong candidates reach the interview stage sooner, before competitors contact them.

Also works for sales

The same workflow can handle sales leads and quote requests: an instant confirmation, an alert to the sales team, and sorting by budget, service or location, so the most valuable leads are followed up first.

Case study 02 · Capstone team project

Customer Feedback Automation

An AI-powered system that scores every piece of customer feedback, sends it to the right place and reports the results on a live management dashboard.

Platform
Make and Airtable
Client
Car care chain with four branches
Speed
11 seconds per run in testing
Built with
MakeOpenAIGoogle SheetsAirtableGmail

The challenge

A car care chain with branches in Manchester, Leeds, Liverpool and London receives more feedback than it can review quickly. Urgent complaints are delayed, good reviews go unused, and managers have no single view of each branch.

How it works

  1. Collect. New feedback starts the Make scenario, which first checks the customer's history.
  2. Score. ChatGPT rates each comment for sentiment and severity.
  3. Route. A router sends it down one of five routes, including ready to post, private queue, manager alert and needs human review.
  4. Act and report. Every item is logged to Google Sheets and the management dashboard. Urgent cases email the manager, and negative feedback comes with an AI-drafted reply.

Result

In testing, each piece of feedback was scored, routed and reported in 11 seconds. The dashboard showed feedback by branch, average sentiment and severity, and where every item was sent.

Business value

  • Customers kept. Urgent complaints reach a manager in seconds, so problems can be fixed before a customer leaves.
  • More reviews. Positive feedback is queued as ready to post, turning happy customers into public reviews that attract new business.
  • Clear oversight. Managers compare branches at a glance and act where it matters most.

Also works for

Any business that collects reviews, surveys or support messages, including restaurants, salons, garages, retailers and service companies.
