Job Application Tracker

Problem
Every application has to be logged, acknowledged, passed to HR and sorted by experience before the right reply goes out. By hand, that's slow and easy to get wrong.
Solution
An n8n workflow triggered by each new application. It skips duplicates, saves the applicant, emails a confirmation, alerts HR, then sorts by experience: Junior (0–2 yrs), Mid-level (3–5) or Senior (6+). Each level gets its own sheet and email, from a kept-on-file reply to an interview invite.
Result
Tested with one applicant per level and rerun live while presenting. Every applicant was logged, emailed and filed to the right sheet.
Platform
n8n · 12 nodes
Tools
n8nGoogle SheetsGmail

Customer Feedback Automation

Problem
A car care chain with branches in Manchester, Leeds, Liverpool and London gets feedback it can't review fast enough. Urgent complaints wait, and managers have no single view of each branch.
Solution
A Make scenario that picks up new feedback, checks the customer's history, and has ChatGPT score its sentiment and severity. A router sends it down one of five routes, such as ready to post, private queue, manager alert or needs human review, logging each to Google Sheets and a management dashboard and emailing the manager when it's urgent.
Result
A test run processed feedback in 11 seconds. The dashboard showed volume by branch, average sentiment and severity, where feedback was routed, and draft replies for negative feedback.
Client type
Car care chain, 4 branches
